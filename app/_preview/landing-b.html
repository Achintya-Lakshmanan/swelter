Philadelphia’s real heat and cold days, replayed hour by hour for 1,560 residents, to show which cooling and warming sites reach the people at risk.

What the simulator shows

How a day is built

First, a real day.

Jul 20, 2019 · the 12th-worst of 206 hot days at PHL since 2010 · heat index 88°F at 6 AM, 107°F at 6 PM (NOAA hourly).

Then, real people’s routines.

1,560 residents from Census ACS tracts, RECS air-conditioning rates and CDC PLACES. Each weekday is a real ATUS diary matched by age and work.

Then, real sites.

The city’s 31 cooling and warming sites with posted hours. A trained model picks the 1, 3 or 5 that reach the most predicted risk.

0.519 h

The heat model’s held-out daily exposure error per resident. Winter: 0.318 h.

0.6 pp

Behaviour fitted to Google mobility 2020–21, off by 0.6 percentage points on held-out 2022 hot days across six categories.

Scores measure agreement with simulated and observed behaviour, not health outcomes. The typical winter-day unseen-weather test did not beat a constant baseline, and the simulator says so on that day. Reachable risk is not prevented exposure.
