What it is, and what it isn’t

A planning sandbox: it compares which real sites could reach the most predicted risk on days that happened. It doesn’t predict real health outcomes, assume anyone attends a site, or verify routes, capacity or current facility conditions.

Data: NOAA, U.S. Census ACS 2024, EIA RECS 2020, CDC PLACES, BLS ATUS 2018–2024, Google Community Mobility Reports, City of Philadelphia. Map © CARTO, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
